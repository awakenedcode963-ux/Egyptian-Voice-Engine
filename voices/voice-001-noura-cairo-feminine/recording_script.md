## Recording setup
- Record in a quiet, treated room with a consistent microphone position.
- Prefer mono WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit PCM; no music, reverb, noise reduction, compression or normalization on the raw reference.
- Keep a steady distance from the microphone and leave a short pause between paragraphs.
- Record three complete takes: natural conversational, warm reassuring, and lightly energetic. Keep each as a separate private file.
