# Voice data, consent and privacy

Obtain explicit, documented permission before using a speaker recording for synthetic speech. Permission must cover synthetic generation, commercial/internal use, distribution channels, territory, duration, retention, revocation, and any third-party model hosting.

A public recording, voice note, employment relationship, or permission for ordinary recording is not automatically permission for synthetic speech.

Keep recordings, signed consent, private weights and client deliverables outside Git. Use access-controlled storage, private consent record IDs, retention limits and SHA-256 provenance. Do not remove model watermarks or provenance markers.

Never present generated speech as a live recording, an uninvolved real person's statement, an endorsement, or another product's official voice. Follow platform disclosure policies and applicable law.
